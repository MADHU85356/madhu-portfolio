T20 Cricket Data Analysis

A notebook-based exploratory analysis of RCB IPL 2024 player and match data, covering data validation, cleaning, feature engineering, and visual comparisons.

PythonPandasNumPyMatplotlibSeaborn

Problem & Goal

Turn ball-by-ball and player-level match data into a cleaner dataset and explore batting, bowling, and team performance patterns.

Workflow

  • Combine three match CSV files in one DataFrame.
  • Validate batting and bowling fields and handle missing values.
  • Create strike-rate, economy-rate, and out-status features.
  • Group data by player, role, match, batting position, and match phase.
  • Use bar, pie, and line charts to explore comparisons and trends.

Visualizations included in the notebook

  • Runs scored by player and share of total runs.
  • Wickets taken and wicket contribution.
  • Strike-rate and economy-rate comparisons.
  • Runs and wickets by match; batting consistency and match phases.

Skills demonstrated

Data cleaning · Feature engineering · GroupBy aggregation · Exploratory data analysis · Chart selection · Analytical storytelling
